Current unscaled polynomial derivatives are used. Raw speed <= yaw_hold_speed
(default .04) uses omega=0, matching existing controller and unified guide.

regularization. Invalid enabled reference returns zero phase capability.
The speed ratios constrain target_scale AND final scale after rise/fall
smoothing, while all existing perception/odom/joint/safety freezes remain.
This is a current-phase VELOCITY envelope, not a guarantee of full reference
acceleration feasibility or physical plant behavior. Native/MPPI actuation
limits and braking/collision checks remain authoritative.
